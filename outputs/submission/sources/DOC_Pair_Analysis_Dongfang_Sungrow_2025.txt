Document: Pair Trade Analysis - Dongfang Electric vs Sungrow 2025
Source: Analyst-compiled public disclosure summary using company annual reports, Q1 2026 updates, and policy research.
Date: 2026-04-30

Pair Structure:
- Long: Dongfang Electric (600875.SH / 01072.HK) - official UBS pool stock
- Short: Sungrow Power Supply (300274.SZ) - same-sector non-pool comparator
- Pair rationale: comply with the stock-pool rule while keeping exposure inside Energy Transition equipment.

Dongfang Electric 2025 profile:
- Revenue: RMB 78.62bn (+12.8% YoY)
- Net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB 3.831bn (+31.11% YoY)
- New orders 2025: RMB 117.25bn (+15.93% YoY)
- Year-end order backlog: RMB 140.31bn
- Strategic focus: grid integration, synchronous condensers, clean power equipment, source-grid-load-storage systems

Sungrow 2025 profile:
- Revenue: RMB 89.18bn (+14.6% YoY)
- Net profit attributable to shareholders: RMB 13.50bn (+22.0% YoY)
- Q1 2026 revenue growth: -18.3% YoY
- Q1 2026 net profit growth: -40.1% YoY
- Strategic focus: PV inverters, energy storage systems, downstream clean-tech power electronics

Core Thesis:
- China's energy transition is shifting from pure capacity buildout toward system reliability and grid infrastructure.
- Dongfang benefits directly from policy-backed grid investment, State Grid spending, and order backlog visibility.
- Sungrow is a strong company, but expectations remain high even as the inverter and storage cycle becomes more competitive.

Why Dongfang on the long side:
- Dongfang is leveraged to the RMB 4 trillion State Grid 15th Five-Year Plan investment cycle.
- The company has visible backlog conversion and policy-backed demand.
- Profit growth in 2025 outpaced revenue growth, indicating operating leverage.
- Dongfang's synchronous condenser breakthrough strengthens the grid-stability angle of the thesis.

Why Sungrow on the short side:
- The short thesis is not that Sungrow is structurally broken; it is that the market still prices it for durable high growth.
- Q1 2026 showed a clear slowdown in both revenue and profit.
- Inverter pricing pressure and storage competition create a setup for multiple compression.
- A premium clean-tech multiple is more fragile than Dongfang's backlog-backed infrastructure earnings profile.

Variant View:
- Consensus still groups energy-transition equipment winners together.
- The differentiated view is that upstream grid-backbone infrastructure should outperform downstream power-electronics names when the bottleneck shifts to system reliability.
- This is not a historical spread-validation trade; it is a forward-looking regime-shift trade.

Catalysts:
- Further State Grid capex and implementation details under the 15th FYP
- Additional Dongfang order wins and backlog updates
- More evidence of Sungrow margin pressure or demand normalization
- Investor rotation from premium clean-tech growth into policy-backed infrastructure

Risks:
- Sungrow re-accelerates on overseas demand or policy stimulus for storage
- Grid capex execution slows or Dongfang backlog conversion disappoints
- Broad clean-tech beta lifts both legs temporarily
