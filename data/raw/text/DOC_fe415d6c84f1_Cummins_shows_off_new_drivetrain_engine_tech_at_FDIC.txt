Cummins is displaying the latest drivetrain and engine technologies for fire and rescue professionals at the Fire Department Instructors Conference (FDIC).
Clarion’s own FDIC International is an annual event for fire and emergency service professionals, featuring hands-on training, educational sessions and the latest technologies. At the event, Cummins is featuring the QuikDisc Rotor, the Cummins X10 diesel engine, and its EPA27 Twin Module Aftertreatment system.
“Fire and emergency vehicles must perform without hesitation,” said Obed Ruiz, General Manager of the Vocational Business at Cummins. “Our engines and drivetrain components are built to handle the extreme demands of emergency response and help ensure first responders can rely on their equipment when every second counts.”
With QuikDisc, Cummins now supports the use of air disc brakes for single rear drive axles at the 33,500 lb capacity rating used by heavy fire apparatus, featuring a two-piece hub design. While traditional rotor replacement on heavy axles requires removing the entire wheel-end, QuikDisc requires only the rotor and its mounting flange to be removed, which can help reduce service time and labor requirements, Cummins argues.
The Cummins X10 on the other hand delivers 450 hp and 1,650 lb-ft of torque fire, with a design based on the applications and demands of heavy- and medium duty trucks. The system also features separate DPF and SCR assemblies meant to enable flexible chassis integration while maintaining serviceability requirements. Additionally, the Cummins electric heating system delivers on-demand thermal management.
