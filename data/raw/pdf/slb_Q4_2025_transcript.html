SLB (SLB) Q4 2025 Earnings Call Transcript

By Motley Fool Transcribing – Jan 23, 2026 at 10:38AM EST

Image source: The Motley Fool.

Date

Friday, January 23, 2026 at 9:30 a.m. ET

Call participants

  • Chief Executive Officer — Olivier Le Peuch
  • Chief Financial Officer — Stephane Biguet
  • Vice President, Investor Relations — James McDonald

Takeaways

  • Revenue -- $9.7 billion, up 9% sequentially, with $817 million total increase including $300 million from an extra month of ChampionX consolidation.
  • Adjusted EPS (excluding charges and credits) -- $0.78, a $0.09 sequential increase, but a $0.14 decrease year over year.
  • Adjusted EBITDA Margin -- 23.9%, up 83 basis points sequentially, with digital performance as the primary driver; margin was constrained by a 50-basis-point loss in carbon capture projects.
  • Free Cash Flow -- $2.3 billion generated in the fourth quarter, while full-year free cash flow was $4.1 billion, marking the third consecutive year above $4 billion.
  • Digital Division Revenue -- $825 million, up 25% sequentially; pretax operating margin expanded by 557 basis points to 34%.
  • Digital Annual Recurring Revenue -- Surpassed $1 billion, up 15% year on year.
  • Reservoir Performance Revenue -- $1.7 billion, up 4% sequentially with a 19.6% pretax operating margin, a 105-basis-point sequential improvement.
  • Well Construction Revenue -- $2.9 billion, down 1% sequentially; pretax operating margin 18.7%, slightly down.
  • Production Systems Revenue -- $4.1 billion, up 17% sequentially; excluding ChampionX, up 11%; pretax operating margin 16%, up 20 basis points.
  • Fourth-Quarter Organic Revenue Growth -- About $500 million, with ChampionX contributing an additional $300 million.
  • International Organic Revenue Increase -- 7% sequentially, with North America organic revenue up 6%.
  • Regional Dynamics -- Middle East (especially Saudi Arabia and UAE) and Asia showed increased activity; Saudi Arabia and Sub-Saharan Africa returned to growth, Mexico remained flat.
  • Full-Year Revenue Guidance for 2026 -- $36.9 billion to $37.7 billion, assuming oil prices in the high-$50s to low-$60s range.
  • ChampionX 2026 Revenue Contribution -- Expected to provide an incremental $1.8 billion to annual revenue.
  • Annual Return to Shareholders -- More than $4 billion planned for 2026 through dividends and share repurchases, including a 3.5% dividend increase.
  • Capital Investments -- $2.5 billion planned for 2026 including CapEx, APS, and exploration data; capital intensity expected to remain at the low end of the 5%-7% of revenue range.
  • First-Quarter 2026 Outlook -- Anticipates high single-digit sequential revenue decline and adjusted EBITDA margin down by 150-200 basis points from the prior quarter due to year-end seasonality.
  • Subsea Market Outlook -- Over 500 subsea tree awards expected across 2026-2027, about 20% higher than the 2025 run rate; aiming to surpass $9 billion in cumulative OneSubsea bookings over two years.
  • Data Center Solutions Business -- Targeting a $1 billion annualized revenue run rate by year-end, with expansion planned internationally.
  • Full-Year 2026 Adjusted EBITDA Guidance -- $8.6 billion to $9.1 billion, with overall margin in line with 2025.
  • Synergy Target from ChampionX Acquisition -- Approximately $400 million targeted, with half expected by 2026 and $30 million realized in 2025; 75% of synergy benefits to production systems.
  • Effective Tax Rate -- Forecasted at approximately 20% for 2026, a slight increase from 2025.

Summary

SLB N.V. (SLB +2.58%) guided for 2026 revenue between $36.9 billion and $37.7 billion, reflecting anticipated benefits from the full-year inclusion of ChampionX and an international market rebound. The company projects total shareholder returns above $4 billion for 2026, driven by continued free cash flow strength and a 3.5% dividend increase. Digital operations posted double-digit growth, with annual recurring revenue surpassing $1 billion and the launch of the Tela AI system and expanded partnerships furthering momentum in this segment. SLB N.V. highlighted strong international growth prospects in the Middle East, Latin America, and Asia, while expecting slight declines in Europe and Africa, and continued flat land activity with growth in North America focused on data center and offshore. The company expects capital investments of $2.5 billion, maintains a capital intensity target of 5%-7%, and sees margin headwinds in well construction and reservoir performance, partly offset by ChampionX synergies and digital expansion.

  • Management said the subsea market may see a "20% higher" tree award run rate in 2026-2027, creating an opportunity for OneSubsea, with 2025 bookings at $4 billion and a cumulative $9 billion targeted over the following two years.
  • Olivier Le Peuch conveyed, "We anticipate this will translate into a higher fourth-quarter revenue exit rate in 2026 compared to the fourth quarter of 2025," pointing to international market recovery as a key cyclical driver.
  • International operations are expected to lead 2026 growth, particularly in The Middle East and Asia, with Saudi Arabia rebounding to early-2025 rig count levels, while North America growth will be weighted to offshore, ChampionX, and data centers.
  • Management emphasized that capital efficiency improvements enable growth at lower capital intensity, but indicated CapEx could increase if activity outpaces expectations, while remaining within the low end of their guidance.
  • SLB N.V.'s data center solutions business, launched less than two years ago, is targeting an exit run rate of $1 billion in annual revenue, planning international expansion and product diversification in 2026.
  • Olivier Le Peuch stated SLB N.V. is currently "the only international service company actively operating in Venezuela," with existing assets, personnel, and customer inquiries positioning the company for rapid ramp-up if conditions allow.
  • Adjusted EBITDA margin for 2026 is forecast "relatively consistent with 2025 levels," with digital and production systems margin expansion partially offset by activity mix and pricing pressures elsewhere.
  • Management described production recovery, including ChampionX, digital, and data center business, as "new pathways for growth supporting a full-year revenue and margin guidance."

Industry glossary

  • ChampionX: An oilfield technology and production-chemical company acquired by SLB N.V., consolidated into SLB's results as of late 2025.
  • APS projects: Asset performance solutions projects, which are outcome-based commercial models where SLB shares in the production uplift and/or cost savings of customer projects.
  • OneSubsea: SLB N.V.'s integrated subsea business, supplying equipment, systems, and services for offshore production and recovery, including subsea trees and related technologies.
  • Subsea tree: An assembly of valves, spools, and fittings installed on a subsea wellhead to control the flow of oil or gas produced from or injected into a well.
  • Lumi/Delphi/Tela: SLB N.V.'s digital and AI platforms for integrating workflow automation, data analytics, and operational intelligence across the upstream oilfield lifecycle.
